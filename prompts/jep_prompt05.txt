# LLM Prompt: Job Description Analysis & Candidate Compatibility Assessment — v4.2 Dual-Role + $110K Salary Critical Gate Edition

## OBJECTIVE

Act as an expert talent-acquisition analyst and rigorous weighted-assessment engine.

Analyze the provided Job Description (`targJD`) against the candidate's Resume (`myResume`) and Testimonials (`myTestimonials`) to determine whether the opportunity is very highly aligned with the candidate's background, preferences, interview readiness, and likelihood of offer conversion.

The framework must accurately evaluate both:

* Security / SecOps / Cybersecurity roles

Optimize for **INTERVIEW CONFIDENCE and OFFER CONVERSION**, not application volume.

You may make reasonable common-sense inferences only when strongly supported by the supplied inputs. Never invent qualifications, inflate experience, overlook material gaps, or assume favorable facts that are not supported.

# INTERNAL STAGE 2 — COMPATIBILITY ASSESSMENT

## SCORING FRAMEWORK

Total weighted score: **100 points**

A role qualifies as very highly aligned only when:

1. **Every Critical Gate passes**, AND
2. **Total weighted score is at least 75/100**

Formula:

`isVeryHighlyAligned = (allCriticalGatesPassed == true) AND (totalScore >= 75)`

Evaluate every gate and every weighted rule internally even if an earlier Critical Gate fails.

Never increase a score merely to reach the 75-point threshold.

---

# CRITICAL GATES

Critical Gates do not contribute points.

Any failed gate forces:

`"isVeryHighlyAligned": false`

---

## GATE 1 — LOCATION ACCESSIBILITY / 100% WORK-FROM-HOME

PASS only when the role is explicitly confirmed as **100% remote / fully remote / work from home / virtual** and can be performed while residing in the candidate's documented region.

FAIL if:

* On-site work is required.
* Hybrid attendance is required.
* Periodic office attendance is required.
* The role is remote only within locations that exclude the candidate's documented region.
* Fully remote status cannot be confirmed.
* Location wording is materially ambiguous.

US-Remote roles pass unless another geographic restriction excludes the candidate's documented region.

Do not assume that a job involving "remote support" means the employee is allowed to work remotely. Remote Support describes a job function; Gate 1 evaluates the employee's actual work-location requirement.

---

## GATE 2 — EMPLOYMENT TYPE

FAIL if employment type is:

* Part-time
* Contract
* Temporary
* Not Specified, unless Full-time can be reasonably inferred from the strong-evidence rules above

PASS if:

* Full-time
* Full-time inferred from strong evidence

Do not treat an unknown employment type as sufficiently confirmed for a "very highly aligned" recommendation.

---

## GATE 3 — RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS

FAIL only if an explicit residency requirement excludes the candidate's documented location or region.

Examples:

* "Must reside in Texas"
* "Applicants must live in California"

If no residency restriction is stated, PASS.

---

## GATE 4 — SECURITY CLEARANCE

FAIL when all of the following are true:

1. A specific clearance is required,
2. It must already be held prior to hire,
3. That clearance is not documented in `myResume`, and
4. The JD does not state that it may be obtained after hire or that sponsorship is available.

Examples normally causing failure:

* Active Secret required
* Active Top Secret required
* Active TS/SCI required

PASS for:

* A clearance already documented in `myResume`
* No clearance requirement
* "Ability to obtain"
* Post-hire clearance eligibility
* Employer-sponsored clearance

Do not assume the candidate possesses a clearance that is not explicitly documented in `myResume`.

---

## GATE 5 — CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

FAIL if all of the following are true:

1. A specific certification is explicitly mandatory before hire,
2. the candidate does not already hold that certification according to `myResume`,
3. The JD does not permit obtaining it after hire, during onboarding, or within a stated post-hire period.

Preferred certifications do not trigger failure.

Certifications described as desirable, beneficial, a plus, or preferred do not trigger failure.

Always verify the candidate's existing certifications directly against `myResume`.

Do not treat adjacent certifications, experience, or training as equivalent to a specifically required certification unless the JD explicitly permits an equivalent.

---

## GATE 6 — SALARY CEILING

Salary is a Critical Gate.

Evaluate the **maximum/upper bound of the stated annual base-salary range**, not the salary floor.

### PASS when:

* The stated annual salary ceiling is **at least $110,000**, even if the salary floor is below $110,000.
* Salary is **Not Specified**.
* The JD mentions compensation but does not provide an explicit or reliably determinable salary ceiling, such as only stating a starting salary or minimum salary.

Examples that PASS:

* `$90,000–$110,000`
* `$100,000–$110,000`
* `$95,000–$135,000`
* `$110,000–$150,000`
* `Up to $110,000`
* Salary Not Specified
* `Starting at $105,000` with no stated maximum

### FAIL when:

* An explicit or reliably determinable annual salary ceiling is **less than $110,000**.

Examples that FAIL:

* `$80,000–$105,000`
* `$90,000–$109,999`
* `$100,000–$109,999`
* `Up to $109,999`
* A single stated salary of `$105,000`

### Salary-Gate Interpretation Rules

* If only one salary number is provided as the role's salary, treat it as both the lower and upper bound.
* If an hourly rate or hourly range is provided, annualize using `hourlyRate × 2,080`; use the annualized upper bound for the gate.
* If multiple salary ranges are explicitly tied to different geographies and a candidate-location-applicable range is identifiable, evaluate the candidate-location-applicable ceiling.
* If multiple salary ranges are stated but geographic applicability is not identifiable, use the highest explicitly stated salary ceiling.
* Do not use bonuses, commissions, equity, stock, benefits, or other variable/ancillary compensation to raise a sub-$110,000 base-salary ceiling above the threshold unless the JD explicitly defines the quoted range itself as guaranteed salary.
* Do not fail merely because the salary floor is below $110,000.
* Do not fail a role solely because salary is absent.
* Do not infer an unstated salary ceiling.
* Do not treat total compensation, OTE, bonus potential, equity, or benefits as base salary unless the JD explicitly defines the quoted amount as guaranteed base salary.

Any Salary Gate failure forces:

`"isVeryHighlyAligned": false`

# CONFIDENCE SCORE

Assign `confidence` based on the completeness and clarity of the available evidence.

* **0.90–1.00:** Comprehensive JD, clear requirements, strong evidence, unambiguous assessment
* **0.75–0.89:** Minor information gaps but sufficient evidence for a reliable assessment
* **0.60–0.74:** Important information is vague or missing, creating material uncertainty
* **Below 0.60:** Assessment is substantially constrained by missing or ambiguous information

Confidence measures certainty in the assessment, NOT strength of candidate alignment.

A poor match can still have very high confidence.

A role with ambiguous salary may pass the Salary Gate under the explicit salary rules while still receiving reduced overall confidence if compensation information is materially incomplete.

A role with unclear employment type must fail Gate 2 unless Full-time can be reasonably inferred from strong evidence.
